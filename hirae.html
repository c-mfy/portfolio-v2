WORK ABOUT

hirae

A mobile app that turns walking into an exploration game. Areas you've physically walked through fill in green and stay filled, building a map of everywhere you've been.

Year
2026
Role
Concept, design & prototype
Tools
Figma, Expo/React Native, Mapbox, Supabase

CHALLENGE/PROBLEM

Most people have no real record of where they've been. Photos are scattered across a camera roll, and map apps show where you are, not where you've already been. Travel logging apps exist, but they ask you to stop and enter things manually, which almost nobody keeps up.

SOLUTION

hirae records passively and rewards you for it. Walk somewhere and that area fills in green on the map permanently. Landmarks unlock by photographing them in person. Walks log themselves as journal entries you can rename and annotate afterwards, so the record builds up without ever asking you to maintain it.

KEY DECISIONS

What "explored" measures. Percent of the world is useless — you'd sit near 0.0004% forever. I scoped progress to your city by area, and made world progress count countries visited instead, out of 195.

How landmark photos verify. Image recognition would confirm what you photographed. I used the photo's EXIF coordinates against the landmark's radius instead. It only proves where you stood, not what you shot, but it's a fraction of the complexity and enough for a walking app.

What counts as a walk. Raw GPS produces constant noise. A session only saves past 150m and three minutes of real movement, and points implying vehicle speed get discarded, so the journal stays clean without the user curating it.

APPROACH/PROCESS

WHERE IT STANDS

Designed end to end in Figma and built as a working prototype in Expo and React Native: location tracking, the area reveal, and the journal all run on a device. It isn't on any app store, yet, but in the future I would love to deploy it to real users.
