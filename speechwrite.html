WORK ABOUT

SPEECHWRITE

A tool for measuring speech recognition bias in accented English. Maps where transcription systems fail different groups of speakers, and by how much.

Year
February 2026
Role
Design & development
Tools
Whisper, Mozilla Common Voice, Figma

CHALLENGE/PROBLEM

Subtitles that can't keep up with a speaker's accent are a familiar annoyance, but the failure is uneven and mostly invisible. Speech recognition systems perform worse for some speakers than others, and without a way to see where and by how much, the gap is hard to argue about or fix.

SOLUTION

SpeechWrite runs Common Voice recordings through Whisper and compares the transcription to the reference at the phoneme level rather than the word level. Word error rate tells you a system got something wrong; phoneme comparison tells you which sounds it lost, which is where accent-specific failure actually lives. Those errors are then aggregated by the speaker's country and dialect and rendered as a global map, so a pattern that would otherwise sit in a results table becomes something you can point at.
