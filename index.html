SPEECHWRITE

I co-built a tool that measures speech recognition bias across 5+ English accent groups, and found error rates 2–5× higher for non-Western accents.

After testing a few ways to score transcription errors, I chose phoneme-level comparison over word error rate, and I was responsible for the analysis pipeline end to end.

I designed the whole thing in Figma and built it as a working prototype in Expo and React Native, including the location tracking, area reveal, and journal.
